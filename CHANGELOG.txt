Warmane BiS Gear: changelog
===========================

1.7.1
  - Fix: after updating from 1.6.0 with /reload instead of a restart, the window could fail
    to open (error "BuildStats (a nil value)"). The Stats tab now lives in the addon's
    existing files, so a /reload is enough. If the Stats data is ever missing, the window
    still opens without that tab instead of breaking.

1.7.0
  - New STATS tab (or /bis stats): your hit, expertise, defense and armor pen caps, worked out
    from your talents and race, next to what the full BiS set has and what you have now.
    Below the caps: spell power, haste, crit, intellect, spirit, strength, agility, stamina ...
    with the BiS set's number as the one to aim for, and how close you are in %.
  - The BiS numbers come from each spec's sixtyupgrades export (gear, gems, enchants and
    talents, no raid buffs). Hover a row to see how the cap is worked out.
  - A tick box counts raid hit: a Draenei in the group (+1%) and Misery or Improved Faerie
    Fire on the boss (+3% spell hit). On by default; untick it for 5-mans or without them.

1.6.0
  - Enchant items next to the gems: the scroll, arcanum, inscription, leg armor,
    spellthread, armor kit, scope or belt buckle that puts each BiS enchant on, shown as an
    icon in front of the gems (237 slots across the 30 specs).
  - Shift-click any item, gem or enchant item with the Auction House open (Browse tab) and
    it searches for it straight away. With the AH closed, Shift-click links it in chat.
  - Enchants that have no item (engineering tinkers, tailoring embroideries, Reticulated
    Armor Webbing, ring enchants, Death Knight runes) only show their name, as before.

1.5.3
  - Unholy Death Knight: talents corrected to 1/17/53. 1 point in Subversion (Blood),
    Unholy Blight removed. Glyphs unchanged.
  - Fire Mage: Lingering Illness (belt) now shows 3 Reckless Ametrine instead of 2.

1.5.2
  - Main hand, off hand and ranged / relic / wand / sigil sit closer together under the
    character. Long weapon names wrap, gems get their own line.

1.5.1
  - The three weapon slots moved to a row centered under the character, like the game's
    own character window.

1.5.0
  - Works with Raids Logs Report's Talents Check: your talents and glyphs are sent to your
    raid or party only (addon prefix WBGT), so the raid leader can see glyphs, which the
    game cannot inspect. Nothing else is sent.
  - Peezy added to the credits.

1.4.0
  - Gems and exact enchants for all 30 specs.
  - <Bastion> banner on top of the window; click it for the Discord link. /bis credits
    prints it too.

1.3.0
  - Gems: small gem icons on each item's enchant line (hover for the gem, Shift-click to
    link). The item tooltip and the dressing room show the exact BiS enchant.

1.2.0
  - Every item shows where it comes from: boss and difficulty, emblem cost, tier token,
    reputation or crafting.

1.1.0
  - 30 specs, every class covered.

1.0.0
  - First release: Gear, Talents and Glyphs tabs, and a "Warmane BiS:" line on item tooltips.
