Warmane BiS Gear 1.7.1
======================

Best in slot gear, talents and glyphs for each spec on Warmane (WotLK 3.3.5a).
Not progressive: there is one list per spec, always the latest phase.

Open it:   /bis   (or /wbis, or the minimap button)
           /bis mage, /bis paladin ...   jump to a class
           /bis stats                    open the Stats tab (your caps and stats to aim for)
           /bis tooltip                  turn the "Warmane BiS:" line on item tooltips on or off
           /bis minimap                  show or hide the minimap button

Resize:    drag the grip in the bottom-right corner. Right-click it to reset.
           The size is remembered.

Tabs
  Gear     your own character in the middle wearing the whole set, items left and right
           with their enchant and where each piece comes from (boss and difficulty,
           emblem cost, tier token, reputation or crafting). Hover for the tooltip, Shift-click to link, Ctrl-click
           for the dressing room. Next to the enchant: the enchant's item (scroll, arcanum, leg armor ...)
           and the gems. With the Auction House open, Shift-click any of them to search it there.
  Talents  all three trees, the BiS ranks highlighted.
  Glyphs   major and minor glyphs.
  Stats    hit / expertise / defense / armor pen caps for your talents and race, the stats the
           BiS set ends up with (the number to aim for) and your own, with how far off you are.

Every item tooltip in the game also gets a line saying which specs list it as BiS,
and where to get it.
The normal-mode copy of a heroic BiS item says "(heroic version)".

Specs in this version (30):
  Warrior:      Fury, Protection
  Paladin:      Holy, Retribution, Protection
  Hunter:       Marksmanship (wtb hands), Marksmanship (wts hands)
  Rogue:        Assassination, Combat (Rupture), Combat (no Rupture)
  Priest:       Discipline, Holy, Shadow
  Death Knight: Frost, Blood (Tank), Unholy
  Shaman:       Elemental, Enhancement, Enhancement (Spellhancer), Restoration
  Mage:         Arcane, Fire, Fire (Spirit)
  Warlock:      Affliction, Demonology
  Druid:        Feral Tank (Bear), Feral DPS (Cat), Balance, Balance (LK), Restoration
